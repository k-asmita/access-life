For Doctors and Hospital Social Workers

Access Life Borivali Center provides a free, safe and hygienic home for underprivileged children undergoing treatment, and their caregivers, so that treatment is not interrupted by lack of a place to stay.

Who can be referred:

  • Underprivileged children undergoing treatment for cancer, blood disorders (such as Thalassemia) or bone marrow transplant
  • Each family: 1 child and 2 primary caregivers
  • Age: children from 0 to 14 years

What the center provides:

  • Free, hygienic accommodation
  • Nutritious meals and a kitchen facility
  • Safe transport to the hospital
  • Education, counselling and recreational activities

Capacity:

18 families (54 beneficiaries). Please contact us to check bed availability before sending a family.
