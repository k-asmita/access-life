Visitor guidelines:
  • Zero-Infection Protocol: The children have severely compromised immune systems. If you have an active cold, cough, fever, runny nose, or any viral symptoms, you must postpone your visit until you are fully recovered.
  • Confidentiality & Privacy: Visitors and volunteers must respect the privacy of the residing patients and their families. Taking pictures or recording videos of the children inside the facility is strictly prohibited unless clear, written authorization is granted beforehand by the operations head.
  • Donation Restrictions: Individual visitors and volunteers are strictly forbidden from directly distributing items (such as sweets, food products, cash, or old clothes) directly to the children or parents. Any material contributions must be coordinated beforehand through the desk manager to ensure they meet health standards.
  • Behavioral Decorum: Guests are expected to maintain an encouraging, positive, and empathetic demeanor, keeping activities gentle, low-stress, and comforting for the recovering bravehearts.
