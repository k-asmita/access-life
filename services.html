100% Free of Cost

Holistic Services & Facilities

Everything a family needs during a child's cancer treatment, under one caring roof – 100% free of cost.

100% Free of Cost

1. Infection-Controlled Hygienic Accommodation

Cancer treatments like chemotherapy drastically lower a child's immunity. Our 18 family units are sanitized twice daily to prevent life-threatening secondary infections.

  • 18 private, airy, well-ventilated family rooms
  • Comfortable bedding, clean linens changed regularly
  • Child-safe, sanitized bathrooms with hot water facility
  • Strict infection control protocols and daily temperature logs
Nutritional Support

2. Nutrition, Rations & Community Kitchen

Good nutrition is critical for a child to endure harsh chemotherapy. We empower mothers to cook familiar, customized meals for their children.

  • Fully equipped modern kitchen with separate stoves and utensils
  • Monthly fresh ration kits (grains, pulses, spices, cooking oil, milk)
  • RO-filtered pure drinking water system
  • Nutritional supplements and fruit distribution
Safe Transit

3. Safe Transportation & Hospital Services for Children

Children undergoing cancer treatment often have weakened immune systems and require frequent hospital visits. Access Life Borivali Centre helps make these journeys safer and more comfortable.

  • 3 Specialized Pediatric Ambulance Services for hospital visits and treatment.
  • 3 Major Children's Hospitals providing medical care and critical care facilities.
  • Regular Hospital Visits for chemotherapy, medical appointments, and follow-ups.
  • Transportation Management coordinated by staff for smooth hospital visits.
  • Vehicle Maintenance Support covering fuel, driver salaries, servicing, and maintenance.
Child Development

4. Non-Formal Education & Skill Learning

Treatment cycles can last 6 months to 2 years. We make sure our brave children don't fall behind in education and intellectual growth.

  • Dedicated study corner with children's library and digital tablets
  • Basic language (Hindi/Marathi/English) and arithmetic coaching
  • Caregiver literacy and digital skill sessions for mothers
  • School re-enrollment guidance post-recovery
Mental Wellbeing

5. Psycho-Social Counselling & Emotional Support

A cancer diagnosis causes severe mental and emotional distress. Our certified counsellors stand beside families every step of the journey.

  • One-on-one therapy sessions for anxious children and stressed parents
  • Peer support group meetings where mothers share coping strategies
  • Art therapy and storytelling to ease medical anxiety
  • Grief, trauma, and resilience counselling

Are You a Family in Need?

Our team is available to assist you with the admission process and support your child's stay during treatment.
