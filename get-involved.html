Volunteer

What Volunteers Do

Volunteers share their time, energy, and skills to bring joy and emotional strength to the children and their caregivers. Opportunities are grouped into the following areas:

1. Educational Support

Teaching foundational subjects like Language, Mathematics, Environmental Science, and General Knowledge.

2. Recreational Activities

Conducting arts and crafts sessions, storytelling, music, and playing games.

3. Health & Well-being

Leading therapeutic sessions such as yoga, stretching, and mindfulness exercises for both children and parents.

4. Emotional & Companionship Support

Interacting with families to offer hope, comfort, and positive engagement during difficult treatment cycles.

5. Creative Collaborations

Organizing special community engagement activities or art projects to brighten up the child-friendly spaces.

When you can volunteer (days / timings): 10:00 AM - 7:00 PM, Monday to Sunday
