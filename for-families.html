For Families

Is your child undergoing treatment for cancer, a blood disorder or a bone marrow transplant? Access Life Borivali Center offers a free, safe and hygienic home for the child and caregivers during treatment.

WHO CAN STAY:

  • Underprivileged children undergoing treatment for cancer, blood disorders (such as Thalassemia) or bone marrow transplant
  • Each family: 1 child and 2 primary caregivers
  • Age: children from 0 to 14 years

WHAT YOU GET (FREE):

  • Hygienic accommodation
  • Nutritious meals and a kitchen facility
  • Safe transport to the hospital
  • Education, counselling and recreational activities

HOW TO APPLY:

  1. Contact us by call or WhatsApp: 808022377 / WhatsApp
  2. Please share your child's treatment details and hospital referral documents with our team for verification.
  3. Our team checks availability and documents
  4. Visit the center and complete registration

FAQ:

Yes, the stay at Access Life is provided free of cost to eligible children undergoing cancer treatment and their families, subject to the center's eligibility criteria and bed availability.

Families can stay at Access Life during their child's cancer treatment, depending on their needs and accommodation availability. The duration of stay may vary based on the treatment plan.

Each family can stay with the child and up to 2 primary caregivers, such as the child's mother and father.

Yes, free meals are provided to children undergoing treatment and their accompanying caregivers during their stay at Access Life.

Yes, transportation to and from the hospital is provided free of cost to help children access their cancer treatment.

What to bring from home: Only clothes.

Yes, visitors are allowed at Access Life, but they must follow the center’s visiting hours and guidelines to ensure a safe and comfortable environment for all children.

Families are expected to maintain cleanliness, follow hygiene and safety guidelines, respect other residents, and follow the center's rules regarding visitors and shared spaces. These rules help provide a safe, clean, and comfortable environment for all children and their families.

If all beds are occupied, families can contact Access Life to check future availability and ask about alternative accommodation options.

You can contact Access Life by phone at +91 8080 222 377 or by email at contact@accesslife.org for information about accommodation, eligibility, and availability.
