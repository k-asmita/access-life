About Access Life – Borivali Center

A loving, hygienic haven offering holistic care to children fighting cancer, blood disorders, and undergoing bone marrow transplants.

About Access Life

Bridging the Gap in Childhood Cancer Care

Access Life Assistance Foundation is an Indian NGO offering a loving, safe, and hygienic temporary home for underprivileged children battling cancer, who travel with their families from across India to Mumbai for treatment.

Without a safe place to stay, families are often forced to sleep on hospital footpaths or abandon life-saving treatment due to infection risks and living costs. The Borivali Center is designed to ensure no child abandons cancer treatment for lack of accommodation.

Capacity & Family Unit Structure

The center can accommodate 18 families (54 beneficiaries). Each family includes 1 child and 2 primary caregivers.

Visit Main Access Life Website

Connected Hospital

Primarily connected with the MCGM Comprehensive Thalassemia Care, Pediatric Hematology-Oncology and Bone Marrow Transplant Centre.

Who We Serve

Underprivileged children undergoing treatment for cancer, blood disorders (such as Thalassemia) and bone marrow transplant, along with their families.
